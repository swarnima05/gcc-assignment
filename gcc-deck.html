Choosing where to build: decision support for first-time GCC setups in India

India has 2,000+ GCCs, yet a company opening its first one still decides mostly on referrals. This deck maps who is affected, which gap matters most, and a first product to close it.

Swarnima · October 2026

1 · Problem: the evidence

Even basic facts disagree, and ways to check are limited

How big is Bengaluru?Vestian counts 487 GCCs; JLL counts 900+ GCC units. Centres and units are not the same thing, and a newcomer may not know.

What firms do today: ask peers for referrals, hire advisors, read provider-written guides and paid reports.

Not yet quantified: the cost of a wrong city or provider choice. That is the first thing to test in user interviews.

Sources: Vestian (therealtytoday.com), ANSR (May 2026), Zinnov-Nasscom GCC Landscape 2026, JLL via flexiple.com/gcc/statistics, Everest Group PEAK Matrix 2025.

Four gaps, ranked by pain and by what can be built now

3 · Solution

GCC Launch Brief: a comparison brief for a company's first India GCC

For: a strategy lead or COO at a firm evaluating its first GCC.

1. InputFunction, headcount, stage, candidate cities, what matters most.
2. Transparent scoreWeighted fit score per city. The method and weights are always visible.
3. EvidenceEach rating links to a dated source and shows its freshness.
4. Open questionsWhat still needs an expert, so the brief never overclaims.

Why first: this decision comes first in the journey, and the other tools (providers, incentives) plug into it later.
